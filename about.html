About GEC Bhuj

The only Government institute in Kachchh district imparting undergraduate and postgraduate engineering courses.

Our Legacy of Excellence

Government Engineering College, Bhuj was established in September 1994 with the vision of providing quality technical education to the students of Kachchh district and beyond. As the only government engineering institute in the region, we have been at the forefront of engineering education for over three decades.

The college is approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi, and is affiliated with Gujarat Technological University (GTU), Ahmedabad. Our campus is spread across a sprawling area providing state-of-the-art infrastructure for academic and research activities.

We take pride in our alumni who have excelled in various fields of engineering, research, entrepreneurship, and public service, contributing significantly to the development of the nation.
