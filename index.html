About Us

Government Engineering College, Bhuj

Established in September 1994, GEC Bhuj is the only Government institute in Kachchh district imparting undergraduate and postgraduate courses in various engineering disciplines.

The college is approved by AICTE, New Delhi, and affiliated with Gujarat Technological University (GTU), Ahmedabad. Our campus serves as a hub for technical excellence, grooming learners to lead global trends in engineering.
